# Go Execution Search Visibility and Backlink Plan

Baseline date: 18 August 2026  
Primary domain: https://goexecution.com/  
Primary market: United States, with Dallas/DFW as the local authority base

## 1. What the current evidence says

### Technical discovery is working

- `https://goexecution.com/` returns HTTP 200.
- `https://goexecution.com/robots.txt` returns HTTP 200 and permits normal search crawlers.
- `https://goexecution.com/sitemap.xml` returns HTTP 200 and currently contains 56 URLs.
- `https://goexecution.com/blog/why-is-my-website-not-ranking-on-google/` returns HTTP 200.
- No `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` header was found on the checked pages.

This means the immediate bottleneck is not an obvious site-wide crawl block. Google Search Console URL Inspection remains the authoritative source for whether each URL is indexed and which canonical Google selected.

### Public visibility and authority are weak

- Public searches for the exact domain, brand plus service, and off-site brand mentions did not surface a reliable Go Execution result in the accessible result set.
- A third-party backlink index reported the following baseline:

| Domain | Authority score | Referring domains | Links |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| goexecution.com | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| atomicdc.com | 34 | 102 | 102 |
| flowforwardmedia.com | 19 | 12 | 12 |
| dfwstrategy.com | 17 | 10 | 10 |
| select-interactive.com | 17 | 10 | 10 |
| dallasseodogs.com | 8 | 2 | 2 |

These numbers are from one public index and must be cross-checked in Ahrefs and Google Search Console before they are treated as final. The direction is still useful: Go Execution has little or no independently detected link authority while even smaller local competitors have links from multiple domains.

## 5. Ninety-day execution plan

### Days 1–14: entity and trust foundation

1. Complete the Google/GSC/Ahrefs baseline above.
2. Verify that the business name, website, phone number, address or service area, and social profiles are consistent everywhere.
3. Create or complete profiles only on legitimate platforms relevant to a digital agency:
   - LinkedIn company page.
   - Clutch company profile; request two genuine client reviews.
   - DesignRush agency profile.
   - GoodFirms agency profile if its review and listing terms are acceptable.
   - Bing Places and Apple Business Connect if the business meets their eligibility rules.
4. Google Business Profile is conditional. Create or retain it only if Go Execution has an eligible real-world location or genuinely meets customers in person. Do not use a virtual office, mailbox, or unstaffed suite.
5. Price Dallas Regional Chamber membership against the actual networking, member-directory, and marketing benefits. Join only if the business will participate; do not buy membership only for a link.

Expected outcome: consistent entity signals and 3–6 legitimate profiles/citations, not dozens of generic directory submissions.

### Days 15–30: real relationship links

1. Identify 5–10 real clients whose work can be publicly attributed to Go Execution.
2. Ask each willing client for one contextual credit from a project, partners, vendors, or news page. Avoid site-wide footer credits.
3. Publish a detailed Go Execution case study for each participating client, then give the client a useful page they can reference.
4. Ask genuine technology, hosting, creative, and marketing partners to add Go Execution to their partner or specialist directory where appropriate.
5. Confirm that every portfolio brand and performance claim can be supported and used with permission before using it in outreach.

Expected outcome: 3–5 new referring domains with direct commercial relevance.

### Days 21–45: create linkable assets

Build assets that contain something other sites cannot get from a generic agency page.

#### Asset A: WordPress-to-Next.js performance migration case study

- Document the architecture change, redirect preservation, and PageSpeed process.
- Include reproducible before/after measurements, test dates, device profile, and limitations.
- Use the current mobile performance work only after the live deployment is measured again.
- Target links from Next.js, WordPress migration, web-performance, and technical SEO publications.

#### Asset B: Dallas small-business website performance benchmark

- Test at least 50 Dallas small-business websites with a disclosed methodology.
- Publish anonymized aggregate LCP, INP/CLS where field data exists, page weight, image weight, and mobile performance distributions.
- Make the dataset or a clean summary table available for journalists and local business publications.

#### Asset C: Website ranking diagnostic checklist

- Turn Blog 1 into a downloadable but ungated checklist plus a browser-readable HTML version.
- Include a decision tree for discovery, crawl, index, relevance, authority, and measurement.
- Cite primary Google documentation and keep it updated quarterly.

Expected outcome: at least one genuinely citable asset ready before large-scale outreach begins.

### Days 31–90: targeted outreach and digital PR

Work in weekly batches of 10 carefully qualified prospects, not mass-email blasts.

1. **Client and partner outreach:** project pages, vendor directories, and co-marketing articles.
2. **Resource-page outreach:** pages already linking to SEO diagnostics, Next.js migrations, or small-business marketing research.
3. **Local business outreach:** Dallas/DFW publications, business associations, entrepreneurship programs, and relevant event organizers.
4. **Expert commentary:** respond to real journalist/source requests where Go Execution can provide an original, attributable answer.
5. **Data-led PR:** pitch one clear finding from the Dallas benchmark instead of pitching the agency itself.
6. **Podcast/newsletter outreach:** target shows and newsletters serving US small businesses, web teams, and marketing leaders.
7. **Design and technology showcases:** submit only original, permission-cleared projects that meet the showcase's quality bar.

Expected 90-day target:

- 12–20 new legitimate referring domains.
- At least 5 links pointing to useful service or editorial pages rather than only the homepage.
- Brand query consistently visible in Google.
- Priority long-tail queries entering the top 50, subject to indexing, competition, and content quality.
- Growth in US non-branded impressions and clicks in Search Console.

This is a target, not a ranking guarantee. Results depend on Google recrawling the site, competitor activity, and the quality of earned coverage.

## 6. Prospect quality gate

Pursue a link only when most of these are true:

- The site is topically or geographically relevant.
- The linking page is indexable, useful, and receives real human traffic.
- The placement is editorial or a truthful company/profile citation.
- The link fits naturally inside the page's content.
- The publisher has a visible editorial identity and contact information.
- The page is not overloaded with unrelated paid links.
- The target URL is the best resource for the reader.

Reject:

- PBNs and expired-domain networks.
- Bulk `DA 50+` packages.
- Paid link insertions presented as editorial links.
- Mass guest-post farms and irrelevant directories.
- Site-wide footer/sidebar links.
- Automated forum/profile/comment links.
- Exact-match anchor requirements.
- Press-release syndication sold as an SEO link package.

Paid placements must use `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow` as required. Anchor text should be natural: the brand, naked URL, article title, or contextual descriptive wording—not a manufactured keyword ratio.
